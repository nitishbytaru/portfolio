# Product

Personal portfolio of Nitish Durga Bytaru, a full stack web developer.

## Audience (assumption, from the site's availability message)
Recruiters and hiring teams filling full stack roles, often scanning quickly and on varied devices.

## Purpose
Show real shipped work and internship experience. The main action is opening a project; resume and email are always reachable.

## Truth rules
Only real numbers and claims from the site's data. Never invent awards, clients, or counts.

## Brand commitments
Visual direction is pinned by the owner's reference image, `ref/image.png`: a framed board of saturated color tiles with a giant wordmark. Single fixed theme, no theme switching.

## Register
Portfolio surfaces: Persuade on Home, Experience on project pages. Platform: web.
